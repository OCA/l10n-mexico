This module links the CFDIs downloaded from the SAT by ``l10n_mx_sat``
with purchase orders.

A new **Bill from SAT** button on confirmed purchase orders opens a
wizard listing the received CFDIs of the vendor that are not yet linked
to a vendor bill. The selected CFDI is attached to the bill created from
the purchase order, so the standard XML import of the accounting app can
process it, and the SAT document keeps a reference to the bill so it
cannot be used twice.

The module also provides the abstract model
``l10n_mx_sat.document.selector.mixin`` so other modules can offer the
same CFDI selection on their own records.

Configuration
=============

Configure the FIEL credentials and the download of received CFDIs in
``l10n_mx_sat`` (**Settings > Companies > SAT** tab).

The vendor must have the RFC of the CFDI issuer in its **Tax ID** field,
and the company RFC must match the receiver of the CFDI.

Usage
=====

1. Open a confirmed purchase order and click **Bill from SAT**.
2. The wizard lists the received CFDIs of the vendor that are pending to
   bill: folio, date, issuer, total, currency and SAT status. Only CFDIs
   in the currency of the order, not cancelled and not linked to another
   bill are proposed. When exactly one CFDI matches the orders total it
   comes pre-selected.
3. Tick the CFDI that corresponds to the order and click **Create
   Bill**. The vendor bill is created from the purchase order lines with
   the XML attached. A warning is shown when the CFDI total does not
   match the orders total.

The **Not billed** filter of **SAT > Documents** lists the received
CFDIs that still have no vendor bill.
