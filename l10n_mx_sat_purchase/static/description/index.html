Mexico - SAT CFDI on Purchase Orders

This module links the CFDIs downloaded from the SAT by l10n_mx_sat with purchase orders.

A new Bill from SAT button on confirmed purchase orders opens a wizard to pick one of the received CFDIs of the vendor that are not yet linked to a vendor bill. The bill can be created in two ways:

  • Whole CFDI: the bill takes everything pending to bill in the purchase orders and the XML is attached to it, so the standard XML import of the accounting app can process it.
  • By lines: each concept of the CFDI is mapped to the purchase order line it bills, with its quantity, unit price and discount. This covers a CFDI that bills part of several purchase orders.

The SAT document keeps a reference to the bill so it cannot be used twice.

The module also provides the abstract model l10n_mx_sat.document.selector.mixin so other modules can offer the same CFDI selection on their own records.

This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning. Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production. More details on development status

Configuration

Configure the FIEL credentials and the download of received CFDIs in l10n_mx_sat (Settings > Companies > SAT tab).

The vendor must have the RFC of the CFDI issuer in its Tax ID field, and the company RFC must match the receiver of the CFDI.

In Purchase > Configuration > Settings > Invoicing > Bills from SAT CFDIs set, for each company:

  • Match: how CFDIs are matched with purchase orders by default, Whole CFDI or By lines. A vendor can have its own value in the Sales & Purchase tab of its form (Match SAT CFDIs), and the user can change it on each bill.
  • Price by lines: whether the bill lines matched by lines take the unit price of the CFDI (default) or the one of the purchase order.
  • Total tolerance: a CFDI is proposed for the purchase orders when its total differs from theirs by this amount at most.
  • Price tolerance (%): a concept of a CFDI is highlighted when its unit price differs from the one of the purchase order line by more than this percentage.
  • Learn vendor codes: when a concept of a CFDI is mapped by hand to a purchase order line, its NoIdentificacion is kept as the Vendor Product Code of the vendor pricelist of the product, so the next CFDI with that code is matched alone.

To match by lines without reviewing each concept, set the code the vendor uses in its CFDIs (NoIdentificacion) as the Vendor Product Code of the vendor pricelist of the product, or keep Learn vendor codes enabled.

Usage

Whole CFDI

  1. Open a confirmed purchase order and click Bill from SAT.
  2. Pick the CFDI in the SAT CFDI field: it searches the received CFDIs of the vendor in the currency of the order that are not cancelled nor linked to another bill, by folio, issuer, total or UUID. When exactly one CFDI has the total of the orders, or is within the total tolerance, it comes pre-selected.
  3. Click Create Bill. The vendor bill is created from the purchase order lines with the XML attached. A warning is shown when the CFDI total does not match the orders total.

By lines

  1. Open the wizard from a purchase order, or from the CFDI with Match with Purchase Orders in SAT > Documents, which takes every purchase order of the vendor that is pending to bill. Purchase orders can be added or removed in the wizard: the mapping already done is kept.

  2. Set Match to By lines. The Concepts tab lists the concepts of the CFDI (vendor code, description, quantity, SAT unit, unit price, amount and taxes) with the purchase order line proposed for each one:

    • by NoIdentificacion, equal to the Internal Reference of the product or to its Vendor Product Code for the vendor;
    • else by description, ignoring case and accents, equal to the description of the purchase order line, the name of the product or its Vendor Product Name;
    • else the only line with the same unit price.

    Among several candidates, the ones with the same unit price go first, then from the oldest order to the newest. A concept that does not fit in the pending quantity of a line is split to take the next ones.

  3. Review the proposal: change the purchase order line or the quantity to bill of each concept, and use Split to bill the rest of a concept in another purchase order line. Concepts whose price differs from the purchase order beyond the tolerance are highlighted. A concept without purchase order line is billed as a line without purchase order, with the product optionally set in the Product column.

  4. Click Create Bill. No purchase order line can exceed its quantity pending to bill. The bill is created in draft with one line per concept: product, taxes and analytic distribution of the purchase order line, quantity of the CFDI and, depending on the configuration, unit price and discount of the CFDI. The purchase orders are its origin, the series and folio of the CFDI its reference and the date of the CFDI its bill date. The XML is attached; a warning tells when the amount to bill differs from the CFDI subtotal and a note in the chatter when the bill total differs from the CFDI total.

When product_unspsc is installed, the quantity and the unit price of a concept are converted from its SAT unit (ClaveUnidad) to the unit of the purchase order line.

The Not billed filter of SAT > Documents lists the received CFDIs that still have no vendor bill.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Matching by lines takes the taxes of the purchase order line, not the ones of the CFDI.
  • Units of measure are only converted when product_unspsc is installed and the SAT unit of the concept has a unit of measure.
  • A bill is created from a single CFDI.
