1. Open a confirmed purchase order and click **Bill from SAT**.
2. The wizard lists the received CFDIs of the vendor that are pending to
   bill: folio, date, issuer, total, currency and SAT status. Only CFDIs in
   the currency of the order, not cancelled and not linked to another bill
   are proposed. When exactly one CFDI matches the orders total it comes
   pre-selected.
3. Tick the CFDI that corresponds to the order and click **Create Bill**.
   The vendor bill is created from the purchase order lines with the XML
   attached. A warning is shown when the CFDI total does not match the
   orders total.

The **Not billed** filter of **SAT > Documents** lists the received CFDIs that
still have no vendor bill.
