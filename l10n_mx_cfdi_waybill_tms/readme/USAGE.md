Link each truck to a Carta Porte vehicle (plate, SCT configuration, permit,
and insurance). On a cargo trip, use **Carta Porte** after the vehicle, driver,
origin, destination, and cargo lines are set.

Fill the SAT product code and unit on each cargo line. Mark hazardous goods
when the shipment requires it. A trailer coupled to the trip needs its plate
and SAT trailer type; that trailer is added to the Carta Porte. A dolly stays
on the trip and is not sent as a remolque.

In Transport settings, require a published Carta Porte before a cargo trip can
start. Passenger trips are unchanged.
