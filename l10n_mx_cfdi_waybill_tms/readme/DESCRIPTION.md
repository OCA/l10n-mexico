CFDI Carta Porte - Transport
===========================

Stamp a Mexican Carta Porte (CFDI type T) from a cargo trip.

A sale split across two trucks produces two waybills. Two sales on one truck
produce one waybill with both cargo lines. SAT product code, unit, hazardous
data, declared value, and packaging stay on the cargo line in this module.

The freight invoice remains the sale of the transport service.
