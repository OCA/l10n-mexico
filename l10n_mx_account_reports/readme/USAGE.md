Open **Accounting \> Reporting \> MIS Reporting \> MIS Reports** and choose the report you need:

- **Balance General**
- **Estado de resultados**
- **Balanza de comprobación anual**
- **Balanza de comprobación mensual**
- **Cuenta por cobrar vencida**
- **Cuenta por pagar**
- **Flujo de efectivo**

Preview, print, or export from the MIS report instance. Aged receivable/payable use aging period columns; set the pivot date as needed.

Additional Mexico PDF reports (including DIOT) are also available under **Accounting \> Reporting \> Mexico**.

Partner-level MIS reports (one row per partner) are under **Accounting \> Reporting \> Mexico**:

- **Aged by partner**
  - Aged Receivable by partner (by move date)
  - Aged Receivable by partner (by due date)
  - Aged Payable by partner (by move date)
  - Aged Payable by partner (by due date)
- **DIOT by partner (previous month)**
