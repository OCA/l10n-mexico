Install this module on a company that uses the Mexican chart of accounts.
The additional taxes and accounts will be automatically added to the chart template.

- **ISH**: Available in customer invoices and vendor bills tax dropdowns.
  Select the rate that corresponds to the state where the lodging is provided.
- **ISN**: Available only in manual journal entries (type "none").
  Use it to record the monthly payroll tax payment.
- **IEPS 200%**: Available in customer invoices and vendor bills.
  Applies to products subject to the 200% IEPS rate.
