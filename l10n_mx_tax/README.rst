This module adds additional Mexican taxes to the accounting
localization:

- **ISH (Impuesto Sobre Hospedaje)**: Tax on Lodging at rates from 0% to
  5% (including half-point increments), for both sales and purchases.
- **ISN (Impuesto Sobre Nómina)**: State Payroll Tax at rates 1%, 2%,
  3%, and 4%, with a dedicated expense account (603.29.01).
- **IEPS 200%**: Special Tax on Production and Services at 200%, for
  both sales and purchases with cash basis accounting.

Configuration
=============

No additional configuration is required. The taxes, accounts, and tax
groups are loaded automatically when the module is installed on a
company using the Mexican localization (chart template "mx").

Usage
=====

Install this module on a company that uses the Mexican chart of
accounts. The additional taxes and accounts will be automatically added
to the chart template.

- **ISH**: Available in customer invoices and vendor bills tax
  dropdowns. Select the rate that corresponds to the state where the
  lodging is provided.
- **ISN**: Available only in manual journal entries (type "none"). Use
  it to record the monthly payroll tax payment.
- **IEPS 200%**: Available in customer invoices and vendor bills.
  Applies to products subject to the 200% IEPS rate.
